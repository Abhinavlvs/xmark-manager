# Support

- Questions and ideas: open a thread in [Discussions](https://github.com/Abhinavlvs/xmark-manager/discussions).
- Bugs: open an [issue](https://github.com/Abhinavlvs/xmark-manager/issues/new?template=bug.md) with your browser and version, the extension version (Options > About) and the steps to reproduce. Do not paste your exported library or any private data.
- Security problems: do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Installing from source and testing: [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md).

xmark-manager is maintained in spare time by one person; replies may take a while.
